Privacy

Your data stays on your device.

The Hub Community doesn't require an account. Nothing you post is tied to your identity unless you choose to share it.

What we store
  • A random device key in your browser's local storage, so you can check the status of your own submissions.
  • The content you submit (text, images, and the community you choose).
  • Anonymous usage counters. No IP addresses, no fingerprints, no tracking across sites.
What we don't do
  • No account signup, no passwords, no email required.
  • No third-party analytics scripts.
  • No selling or sharing data with advertisers.
  • No tracking you across other websites.
