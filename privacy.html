Privacy

Your data stays on your device.

The Hub Community doesn't require an account. Nothing you post is tied to your identity unless you choose to share it.

What we store
  • Submission IDs and private marketplace management codes in this browser's local storage. They are available only on this device unless you copy them elsewhere.
  • A one-way hash of each new marketplace management code is stored with its listing so only someone with the private code can request a sold update.
  • The content you submit (text, images, and the community you choose).
  • Anonymous usage counters. No IP addresses, no fingerprints, no tracking across sites.
What we don't do
  • No account sign-in or email required.
  • No third-party analytics scripts.
  • No selling or sharing data with advertisers.
  • No tracking you across other websites.
