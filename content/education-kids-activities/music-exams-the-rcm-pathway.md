# Music Exams: The RCM Pathway

<div class="lh-content-body">

<div class="lh-section">


<div class="lh-text">
<p><strong>About the Royal Conservatory of Music (RCM)</strong><br>The RCM offers a structured system of music examinations from beginner to advanced levels, including the Associate Diploma (ARCT).</p><p><strong>Levels and Fees (2026)</strong></p><ul><li><p><strong>Practical Exams:</strong> Fees range from <strong>$75** for Preparatory levels to **$510</strong> for Level 10. ARCT Performer exams cost <strong>$850</strong>.</p></li><li><p><strong>Theory Exams:</strong> Required as co-requisites for higher practical levels. Fees range from <strong>$149** for Level 5 Theory to **$291</strong> for ARCT Harmony &amp; Counterpoint.</p></li></ul><p><strong>Registration &amp; Exam Formats</strong></p><ul><li><p><strong>Registration:</strong> Registration opens and closes on specific dates for each exam session (e.g., Fall, Winter, Spring). Deadlines are strict.</p></li><li><p><strong>Exam Formats:</strong> Practical exams involve a live performance before an adjudicator. Theory exams are written tests.</p></li></ul><p><strong>Official Link:</strong> <a href="https://rcmusic.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">rcmusic.com</a></p><p><em>Last Verified: October 26, 2026</em></p><!--EndFragment-->
