# French Immersion, AP, and IB Programs

<div class="lh-content-body">

<div class="lh-section">


<div class="lh-text">
<p><strong>Who These Programs Suit</strong></p><ul><li><p><strong>French Immersion (FI):</strong> For students who want to achieve a high level of proficiency in French. Entry points are typically in <strong>Grade 1 (Early FI)</strong> or <strong>Grade 4 (Middle FI)</strong>.</p></li><li><p><strong>Advanced Placement (AP):</strong> For motivated students seeking university-level coursework and potential credit. Access is often via a <strong>competitive application prior to Grade 9</strong>, involving report card review.</p></li><li><p><strong>International Baccalaureate (IB):</strong> A rigorous, internationally recognized diploma program. Entry can be competitive, with application points in <strong>Grade 8</strong> (for the Grade 9 preparatory program) and <strong>Grade 10</strong> (for the Diploma Programme).</p></li></ul><p><strong>Enrollment Process &amp; Timelines</strong></p><ul><li><p><strong>French Immersion:</strong> Enrollment is typically based on elementary school registration in the FI track. Access to secondary FI is generally based on elementary enrollment in the respective French program.</p></li><li><p><strong>AP &amp; IB:</strong> These programs require a <strong>competitive application</strong>. For IB, applications for September 2026 were open from November 24 to December 12, 2025, in some boards, with offers sent in February 2026.</p></li><li><p><strong>Key Advice:</strong> Application timelines and entry points vary significantly by school board. <strong>It is critical to check your specific school board's website</strong> for exact dates and requirements.</p></li></ul><p><em>Last Verified: October 26, 2026</em></p><!--EndFragment-->
