# Youth Activities Beyond School

<div class="lh-content-body">

<div class="lh-section">


<div class="lh-text">
<p><strong>Overview</strong><br>Ontario offers a wide range of extracurricular activities that help youth develop skills, make friends, and engage with the community.</p><p><strong>Scouts Canada</strong></p><ul><li><p><strong>Ages:</strong> Programs for youth aged <strong>5 to 26</strong> (Beaver Scouts: 5-7, Cub Scouts: 8-10, Scouts: 11-14).</p></li><li><p><strong>Activities:</strong> Outdoor education, camping, leadership, and community service.</p></li><li><p><strong>Registration:</strong> Registration for the 2026-2027 scouting year opens in <strong>June 2026</strong> for new members. Register at <strong><a href="https://scouts.ca/join" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">scouts.ca/join</a></strong>.</p></li></ul><p><strong>Sports, Arts, and Volunteering</strong></p><ul><li><p><strong>Municipal Recreation:</strong> Most cities and towns offer registered sports leagues, swimming lessons, and arts programs through their community services departments. Check your local municipality's website.</p></li><li><p><strong>Sports Organizations:</strong> Look for local minor hockey, soccer, baseball, and other sport associations.</p></li><li><p><strong>Arts and Culture:</strong> Community centers and private studios offer classes in visual arts, dance, drama, and music.</p></li><li><p><strong>Volunteering:</strong> Many organizations, such as food banks, community centers, and hospitals, offer volunteer opportunities for youth. This is an excellent way to gain experience and give back.</p></li></ul><p><em>Last Verified: October 26, 2026</em></p><!--EndFragment-->
