## HOW TO APPLY

1. **Learn about programs** — Explore pathways and attend information nights.
2. **Check eligibility and boundaries** — Confirm correct school and board for your address.
3. **Check transportation** — Confirm routes, bus eligibility, and travel time before applying.
4. **Prepare documents** — Gather Grade 7/8 reports and requested materials.
5. **Submit application** — Use Peel RLCP or DPCDSB school form by deadline.
6. **Complete assessments** — Follow program-specific instructions if required.
7. **Check offers and respond** — Accept by stated deadline and complete registration.

## IMPORTANT NOTES

- Dates and open houses may change; always check school and board websites.
- AP / IB labels include Grade 9 preparatory pathways.
- Fees, eligibility, and assessments vary by school.
- Regional Arts programs are not included in this summary.
- Public and Catholic boards have separate admission processes.
