HOW TO APPLY (STEP-BY-STEP)1. Learn about programs — Explore pathways and attend information nights.2. Check eligibility &amp; boundaries — Check the correct school and board for your address and confirm transportation before applying.3. Prepare required documents — Gather Grade 7/8 reports and requested materials.4. Submit application — Apply through your board portal (Peel RLCP) or school form (DPCDSB) by its deadline.5. Complete assessment, if required — Follow program-specific instructions.6. Check offers and respond — Accept by the stated deadline and complete registration.
IMPORTANT NOTES• Dates and open houses may change; always check each school and board website for the latest information.• AP / IB labels include Grade 9 preparatory pathways.• Fees, eligibility, and assessments vary by school.• Regional Arts programs are not included in this summary.• Public and Catholic boards have separate admission processes.
