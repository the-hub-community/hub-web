# Education & Kids' Activities

<div class="lh-content-body">

<div class="lh-section">


<div class="lh-text">
<p>A guide for parents to navigate the Ontario school system, from provincial assessments to specialized programs, music exams, cadets, and extracurricular activities.</p><p>As a parent, navigating the Ontario school system can be a daunting task. With so many different programs and activities available, it can be difficult to know where to start. In this guide, we will provide an overview of the key components of the Ontario school system, including provincial assessments, specialized programs, music exams, cadets, and extracurricular activities.</p><p>**Provincial Assessments**</p><p>In Ontario, students are required to participate in provincial assessments in certain subjects. These assessments are designed to measure student learning and provide a snapshot of student achievement. The provincial assessments are typically administered in the spring of each year and include:</p><p>• Mathematics<br>• Reading, Writing, and Mathematics (Grades 3 and 6)<br>• Science and Technology (Grades 9)<br>• Social Studies (Grades 10)</p><p>**Specialized Programs**</p><p>In addition to the regular curriculum, Ontario schools offer a range of specialized programs that cater to students with diverse interests and abilities. Some of the specialized programs available include:</p><p>• French Immersion<br>• Extended French<br>• English as a Second Language (ESL)<br>• Special Education programs for students with special needs<br>• Gifted and Talented programs for students who require a more challenging curriculum</p><p>**Music Exams**</p><p>For students who are interested in music, Ontario schools offer a range of music programs and exams. These programs and exams are designed to provide students with the opportunity to develop their musical skills and to prepare for post-secondary music programs. Some of the music programs and exams available include:</p><p>• Royal Conservatory of Music (RCM) exams<br>• Ontario Music Festival Association (OMFA) exams<br>• Music theory and history courses</p><p>**Cadets**</p><p>For students who are interested in leadership and community service, Ontario schools offer a range of cadet programs. These programs are designed to provide students with the opportunity to develop their leadership skills and to participate in community service projects. Some of the cadet programs available include:</p><p>• Army Cadets<br>• Navy League Cadets<br>• Air Cadets</p><p>**Extracurricular Activities**</p><p>In addition to the specialized programs and music exams, Ontario schools offer a range of extracurricular activities that cater to students with diverse interests and abilities. Some of the extracurricular activities available include:</p><p>• Sports teams (e.g. basketball, soccer, hockey)<br>• Clubs (e.g. debate, drama, robotics)<br>• Volunteer opportunities (e.g. food banks, animal shelters)<br>• Leadership programs (e.g. student council, peer mentoring)</p>
