# Understanding Ontario School Assessments (EQAO & OSSLT)

<div class="lh-content-body">

<div class="lh-section">


<div class="lh-text">
<p><strong>What is EQAO?</strong><br>The Education Quality and Accountability Office (EQAO) administers provincial assessments in <strong>Reading, Writing, and Mathematics</strong> for students in <strong>Grades 3 and 6</strong>, and <strong>Mathematics</strong> for <strong>Grade 9</strong>. These assessments measure how well students are meeting provincial curriculum expectations and focus on problem-solving, critical thinking, and application of knowledge.</p><p><strong>What is the OSSLT?</strong><br>The Ontario Secondary School Literacy Test (OSSLT) is a <strong>Grade 10</strong> assessment that focuses on literacy skills across all subjects. <strong>Successfully completing the OSSLT is a graduation requirement</strong> for the Ontario Secondary School Diploma (OSSD).</p><p><strong>Key Details for Families</strong></p><ul><li><p><strong>Format:</strong> The OSSLT is computer-based and consists of two sessions. It is scored on a scale of 200 to 400, with <strong>300 being the pass mark</strong>.</p></li><li><p><strong>Preparation:</strong> EQAO offers online practice tests and hosts live webinars for parents. Schools may also provide preparation materials.</p></li><li><p><strong>Results:</strong> EQAO assessments are <strong>separate from regular classroom evaluation</strong> and do not directly affect report card grades.</p></li></ul><p><strong>Official Link:</strong> <a href="https://eqao.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">eqao.com</a></p><p><em>Last Verified: October 26, 2026</em></p><!--EndFragment-->
