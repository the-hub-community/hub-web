# Cadets from Age 12

<div class="lh-content-body">

<div class="lh-section">


<div class="lh-text">
<p><strong>Program Overview</strong><br>The Cadet Program is a free, federally funded youth development program for young Canadians aged <strong>12 to 18</strong>. It offers three branches: <strong>Sea, Army, and Air Cadets</strong>.</p><p><strong>Eligibility &amp; Costs</strong></p><ul><li><p><strong>Eligibility:</strong> Must be aged 12–18, a Canadian citizen or legal resident, and have provincial health insurance.</p></li><li><p><strong>Costs:</strong> The program is <strong>free</strong>. Uniforms and equipment are provided at no charge. There is no registration fee.</p></li></ul><p><strong>Step-by-Step Enrollment Process</strong></p><ol start="1"><li><p><strong>Find a Local Unit:</strong> Use the <strong>Corps and Squadron Directory</strong> on the Cadets Canada website to find a unit near you.</p></li><li><p><strong>Complete the Online Application:</strong> Visit the Government of Canada's Cadets website and complete the online application form (CF1158).</p></li><li><p><strong>Submit &amp; Confirm:</strong> The local corps/squadron personnel will confirm receipt of your application.</p></li><li><p><strong>Approval:</strong> The Commanding Officer of the unit gives final approval.</p></li></ol><blockquote><p><strong>Confirmation Step:</strong> The user brief notes to "Confirm details with local corps/squadron," which is essential as schedules and specific unit availability can vary.</p></blockquote><p><strong>Official Link:</strong> <a href="https://canada.ca/en/department-national-defence/services/cadets-junior-canadian-rangers/cadets.html" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer">canada.ca/en/department-national-defence/services/cadets-junior-canadian-rangers/cadets.html</a></p><p><em>Last Verified: October 26, 2026</em></p><!--EndFragment-->
