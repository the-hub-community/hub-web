# Math and Science Contests

<div class="lh-content-body">

<div class="lh-section">


<div class="lh-text">
<p><strong>Common Programs &amp; Grade Ranges</strong></p><ul><li><p><strong>Canadian Mathematics Competition (CEMC, University of Waterloo):</strong> Offers contests for a wide range of grades.</p><ul><li><p><strong>Gauss (Grades 7-8)</strong></p></li><li><p><strong>Pascal (Grade 9), Cayley (Grade 10), Fermat (Grade 11)</strong></p></li><li><p><strong>Euclid (Grade 12)</strong></p></li><li><p><strong>Canadian Intermediate/Senior Mathematics Contest (Grades 9-10, 11-12)</strong></p></li></ul></li><li><p><strong>Canadian Mathematical Society (CMS):</strong></p><ul><li><p><strong>Canada Lynx Mathematics Competition:</strong> Recommended for Grades 7 and up.</p></li><li><p><strong>Canadian Jay Mathematics Competition (CJMC):</strong> For students in <strong>Grade 8 or below</strong>.</p></li></ul></li><li><p><strong>Math Kangaroo:</strong> For students in <strong>Grades 1 through 12</strong>.</p></li><li><p><strong>STEMZ Competition (Professional Engineers Ontario):</strong> For students in <strong>Grades 9-12</strong> (Junior and Senior levels).</p></li></ul><p><strong>Registration &amp; Preparation</strong></p><ul><li><p><strong>Registration:</strong> Typically done through your child's school. Schools register students and pay the contest fees (e.g., $6-$18 per contest).</p></li><li><p><strong>Preparation:</strong> Past contests and practice problems are often available on the organizing bodies' websites (like CEMC) and are an excellent way to prepare.</p></li></ul><p><em>Last Verified: October 26, 2026</em></p><!--EndFragment-->
