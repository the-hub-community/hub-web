Heads up if you like free learning - NASA is running a free online course, and it comes with a free certificate.

NASA Open Science Essentials
Free, online, self-paced, about 2 hours, 5 short lessons.
It covers what open science is, why it matters, open data, open-source software, open results, FAIR data standards, ORCID and DOI, open licensing, and practical tools.
There is also a deeper course, Open Science 101, about 12 hours, 5 modules, if you want the full version.

What you get
Free digital certificate and a NASA digital badge when you finish.
The badge is issued through Credly and can be linked to your ORCID profile.
No tuition, no registration fee, no fee for the certificate or badge. NASA never charges for this training, so do not pay anyone who asks for a fee to secure a seat.

Who can join
Open to the public, worldwide. Students, researchers, professionals, and the general public.
High school to postdoctoral level, all academic disciplines, no science degree needed.
No citizenship restriction for the public courses.

Dates to know
Open Science 101 registration ends Dec 1, 2026.
Open Science Essentials registration ends Dec 2, 2026.
Both are open now until maximum capacity is reached, so do not leave it to the last day.

How to apply
1. Create an account on NASA STEM Gateway.
2. Find Open Science Essentials, or Open Science 101, and click Apply / Register.
3. Wait for the enrollment email, usually 24-48 hours, with instructions to access the course.
4. Finish all lessons / modules and the assessments at your own pace. Your progress is saved between sessions.
5. Claim your NASA digital badge and download your certificate when you finish.

Check it yourself first
