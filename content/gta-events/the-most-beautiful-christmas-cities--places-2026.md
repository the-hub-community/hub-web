# The Most Beautiful Christmas Cities & Places 2026

<div class="lh-content-body">

<div class="lh-section">


<div class="lh-text">
Last checked: Nov 23, 2026. The most beautiful Christmas cities and lights places in Canada and the USA for the 2026 holiday season. Check each official link before you go.
**CANADA**

- Quebec City — German Christmas Market — Nov 19–Dec 23, 2026, Thu–Sun (Thu–Sat 11 AM–9 PM, Sun 11 AM–6 PM; extra days Dec 21–23), 6 sites across Old Quebec incl. Place d'Armes beside Château Frontenac — FREE.
[https://www.mnaq.ca/en/program/](https://www.mnaq.ca/en/program/)

- Montreal — Noël dans le parc (Christmas in the Park) — Dec 4–25, 2026, Parc La Fontaine — FREE.
[https://noeldansleparc.com](https://noeldansleparc.com)

- Vancouver — Canyon Lights at Capilano Suspension Bridge Park — Nov 20, 2026–Jan 10, 2027, daily 11 AM–10 PM (last entry 9 PM), closed Dec 25, 3735 Capilano Road, North Vancouver — included with park admission, timed entry required.
[https://www.capbridge.com/seasonal-events/](https://www.capbridge.com/seasonal-events/)

- Ottawa — Winter Lights Across Canada — Dec 7, 2026–Jan 7, 2027, every evening, Parliament Hill and Confederation Boulevard; plus Ottawa Christmas Market, weekends Nov 20–Dec 20, Lansdowne Park, 60+ vendors.
[https://www.canada.ca/en/canadian-heritage/campaigns/winter-lights.html](https://www.canada.ca/en/canadian-heritage/campaigns/winter-lights.html)

[https://www.ottawachristmasmarket.com/](https://www.ottawachristmasmarket.com/)

- Banff — Banff Christmas Market — weekends Nov 27–29, Dec 3–6, Dec 10–13, 2026 (Thu 5–9 PM, Fri 11 AM–9 PM, Sat 10 AM–9 PM, Sun 10 AM–6 PM), Banff Train Station, 327 Railway Ave — ticketed, pre-purchased tickets required; plus the free In Search of Christmas Spirit light walk, select evenings to Jan 3, Cascade of Time Garden.
[https://banffchristmasmarket.com/](https://banffchristmasmarket.com/)

[https://www.banfflakelouise.com/events/christmas/christmas-spirit](https://www.banfflakelouise.com/events/christmas/christmas-spirit)

- Jasper — "Christmas in Jasper" town programme — Nov 13, 2026–Jan 3, 2027: Festival of Trees Nov 27–Dec 12, Christmas Artisan Market Nov 29, Ski With Santa Dec 19, 20 & 25, Letters to Santa to Jan 3.
[https://www.jasper.travel/christmas-in-jasper/christmas-events-and-workshops](https://www.jasper.travel/christmas-in-jasper/christmas-events-and-workshops)

- Whistler — Wintersphere — Dec 19, 2026–Jan 2, 2027, 10 AM–5 PM daily, closed Dec 25, Whistler Conference Centre — FREE; plus Winterlight, Nov 27–29, Whistler Olympic Plaza — free.
[https://www.whistler.ca/parks-recreation-culture/festivals-and-events/new-years-eve-in-whistler/](https://www.whistler.ca/parks-recreation-culture/festivals-and-events/new-years-eve-in-whistler/)

- Blue Mountain — Holiday Magic Light Trail — Nov 20, 2026–Jan 3, 2027, 1 km light trail lit nightly dusk–10 PM, Blue Mountain Village, 156 Jozo Weider Blvd — FREE, no tickets required.
[https://www.bluemountainvillage.ca/blumination-light-trail](https://www.bluemountainvillage.ca/blumination-light-trail)

- Victoria — The Magic of Christmas at The Butchart Gardens — Dec 1, 2026–Jan 6, 2027, gates 3–9 PM daily, viewing until 10 PM, closed Dec 25, 800 Benvenuto Avenue, Brentwood Bay — adult $41, youth (13–17) $20.50, child (5–12) $5, tax included.
[https://butchartgardens.com/christmas/](https://butchartgardens.com/christmas/)

**USA**

- Tulalip Lights & Ice — Tulalip Resort Casino, Marysville, WA — Nov 21, 2026–Jan 10, 2027, daily, Tulalip Amphitheatre, 10400 34th Ave NE — FREE to view the lights (10M+ lights, outdoor ice rink, holiday marketplace); ice skating paid (from $15 adult / $12 child, skate rental included).
[https://www.quilcedavillage.com/TulalipLightsAndIce](https://www.quilcedavillage.com/TulalipLightsAndIce)

- Longwood Gardens — "A Longwood Christmas" — Nov 20, 2026–Jan 10, 2027, daily 10 AM–11 PM, 1001 Longwood Road, Kennett Square, PA — timed tickets required, adults $45 / $52 peak, youth (5–15) $25 / $32, ages 4 and under free.
[https://longwoodgardens.org/gardens/our-seasons/longwood-christmas](https://longwoodgardens.org/gardens/our-seasons/longwood-christmas)

- Rockefeller Center — Christmas Tree + Saks Fifth Avenue Holiday Light Show — tree lighting Wed Dec 2, 2026, tree on view through mid-January 2027; Saks show from Mon Nov 23 through the holiday season, Rockefeller Plaza, New York — FREE to view.
[https://www.rockefellercenter.com/magazine/events/christmas-at-rockefeller-center-magical-experiences/](https://www.rockefellercenter.com/magazine/events/christmas-at-rockefeller-center-magical-experiences/)

- Callaway Gardens — "Fantasy in Lights" — Nov 13, 2026–Jan 3, 2027, show from 6 PM nightly, Robin Lake Beach, 17800 US-27, Pine Mountain, GA — ticketed, from $19.99 on Value Nights, ages 3 and under free; 7-mile drive, 18 scenes, 10M+ lights.
[https://www.callawayresort.com/experience/seasons-of-callaway/fantasy-in-lights/](https://www.callawayresort.com/experience/seasons-of-callaway/fantasy-in-lights/)

- The Grotto, Portland — "Christmas Festival of Lights" — Nov 27–Dec 30, 2026, entry 5–9:30 PM nightly, closed Christmas Day, 8840 NE Skidmore St, Portland, OR — online $17.95 ages 12+, $8.95 ages 3–11, ages 2 and under free.
[https://thegrotto.org/christmas/](https://thegrotto.org/christmas/)

- Biltmore Estate — "Christmas at Biltmore" — Nov 3, 2026–Jan 10, 2027, 1 Lodge Street, Asheville, NC — ticketed, daytime House & Grounds from $110; Candlelight Christmas Evenings from $135 (separate ticket, reservation only).
[https://www.biltmore.com/news-release/christmas-at-biltmore-2026/](https://www.biltmore.com/news-release/christmas-at-biltmore-2026/)
