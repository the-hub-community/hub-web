# Niagara Falls Winter Festival of Lights 2026–27

<div class="lh-content-body">

<div class="lh-section">


<div class="lh-text">
**Last checked: Nov 21, 2026**
**Canada's largest free outdoor light festival is back — more than 3 million lights, 75+ displays and fireworks over the Falls, every night from November 14, 2026 to January 10, 2027.**
**WHEN &amp; WHERE**- **Dates: November 14, 2026 – January 10, 2027**
- **Lights on nightly: 5:00 PM – midnight, including holidays**
- **Admission: Free**
- [https://www.niagaraparks.com/events/winter-festival-of-lights/](https://www.niagaraparks.com/events/winter-festival-of-lights/)
- **Where: Niagara Parkway, Queen Victoria Park, Dufferin Islands, Clifton Hill, Fallsview District and Lundy's Lane, Niagara Falls, Ontario**
- [https://www.niagarafallstourism.com/wfol/](https://www.niagarafallstourism.com/wfol/)

**THE LIGHTS**- **More than 3 million lights and 75+ displays and interactive installations across an 8 km route**
- [https://www.niagarafallstourism.com/wfol/](https://www.niagarafallstourism.com/wfol/)
- **Walk or drive the illuminated route along the Niagara Parkway, from Clifton Hill to Dufferin Islands**
- [https://www.niagaraparks.com/events/winter-festival-of-lights/](https://www.niagaraparks.com/events/winter-festival-of-lights/)
- **Dufferin Islands — light displays on the islands along the route**
- [https://www.cliftonhill.com/falls_blog/winter-festival-of-lights-in-niagara-falls/](https://www.cliftonhill.com/falls_blog/winter-festival-of-lights-in-niagara-falls/)
- **Falls Illumination: a five-minute choreographed light show on the Canadian Horseshoe Falls nightly at 6:00 PM, 7:00 PM and 8:00 PM**
- [https://www.cliftonhill.com/falls_blog/winter-festival-of-lights-in-niagara-falls/](https://www.cliftonhill.com/falls_blog/winter-festival-of-lights-in-niagara-falls/)

**FIREWORKS OVER THE FALLS**- **Fridays and Saturdays at 8:00 PM, November 14 – December 12, 2026**
- **Nightly at 8:00 PM, December 18, 2026 – January 2, 2027**
- **Final shows: January 8 and 9, 2027, at 8:00 PM**
- **New Year's Eve, December 31: fireworks at 8:00 PM plus an extra nine-minute show at midnight**
- [https://www.niagaraparks.com/events/winter-festival-of-lights/](https://www.niagaraparks.com/events/winter-festival-of-lights/)

**MORE TO DO**- **Weekend entertainment at the Queen Victoria Park Festival Hub: live family entertainment, Santa visits and roaming performers, Fridays and Saturdays 6:00–8:00 PM, November 14 – December 19**
- [https://www.niagaraparks.com/events/winter-festival-of-lights/](https://www.niagaraparks.com/events/winter-festival-of-lights/)
- **Hot Chocolate Trail: Cinnamon Bun Hot Chocolate at Queen Victoria Place Café and Table Rock House Restaurant**
- [https://www.niagaraparks.com/events/winter-festival-of-lights/](https://www.niagaraparks.com/events/winter-festival-of-lights/)
- **New Year's Eve live outdoor concert with country music star Riley Green at the Falls on December 31, with fireworks at 8:00 PM and midnight**
- [https://www.niagaraparks.com/events/winter-festival-of-lights/](https://www.niagaraparks.com/events/winter-festival-of-lights/)
