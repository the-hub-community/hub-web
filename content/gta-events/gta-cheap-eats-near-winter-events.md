Information only. This page shares where to eat near winter events in the GTA, added by users, for users.

Machida Shoten
Japanese ramen on College Street. What to try: the Original ramen.
Price: $14.99.
Address: 326 College St, Toronto.

MAD MOMO &amp; Hakka
Nepalese momos and Hakka dishes in Scarborough. What to try: steamed momos.
Inexpensive.
Address: 2606 Eglinton Ave E, Scarborough.

Habibz Corner
Halal food on Queen Street West, open late. What to try: halal street food favourites.
Inexpensive.
Address: 578 Queen St W, Toronto.

MOBA

Indian food in Trinity-Bellwoods. What to try: the 5-course tasting menu — Veg $69 / Non-veg $79. Also try Chèvre Kebab and Guchhi Khumb Risotto.

Address: 869 Dundas St W, Toronto.

Website: https://www.mobarestaurant.com/

Map: https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&amp;query=MOBA+869+Dundas+St+W+Toronto
