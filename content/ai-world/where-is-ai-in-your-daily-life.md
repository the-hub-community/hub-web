# Where is AI in your daily life?

<div class="lh-content-body">

<div class="lh-section">


<div class="lh-text">
You might not realize it, but you probably interact with AI dozens of times a day.

Here are a few common examples:

- **Your Email Spam Filter**: When your email automatically sorts out junk mail, AI is analyzing the text, the sender, and the links to guess if it’s a scam.
- **Voice Assistants**: When you ask Siri or Alexa to “set a timer,” it uses AI to understand your voice and translate it into a command.
- **Navigation Apps**: When Google Maps reroutes you around a sudden traffic jam, it uses AI to analyze the movement of thousands of other phones in real-time.
- **Customer Service Chatbots**: When you go to a website and a little bubble pops up saying, “Hi, how can I help?”, you are usually talking to an AI trained to answer common questions.
- **Your Camera App**: Modern phones use AI to detect faces, sharpen blurry text, and automatically improve your photos when you hit the shutter button.
