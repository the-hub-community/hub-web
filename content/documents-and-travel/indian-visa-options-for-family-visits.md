# Indian Visa Options for Family Visits

<div class="lh-content-body">

<div class="lh-section">


<div class="lh-text">
<p><b>Overview of Visa Types</b></p><p>The Indian government offers various visa types for different purposes and durations of travel. </p><p><ul><li><b>e-Tourist Visa (30 days):</b> For short trips, holidays, and visiting friends/relatives. Double entry.</li><li><b>e-Tourist Visa (1 year):</b> For multiple leisure trips. Multiple entry; Canadian citizens are commonly allowed up to 180 days per visit.</li><li><b>e-Tourist Visa (5 years):</b> For frequent tourism or family visits. Multiple entry.</li><li><b>e-Family Visa:</b> Specifically for dependents travelling with a student or visiting a close relative who is an Indian citizen or OCI cardholder.</li><li><b>Regular (Paper) Visa: </b>Required for purposes like work, long-term study, journalism, or missionary work.</li></ul></p><p>How to Apply</p><p>To apply for an Indian eVisa, follow these steps:</p><p>1. <b>Determine Your Visa Type:</b> Choose the category that best fits your purpose and duration of travel.<br>2. <b>Apply Online:</b> Complete the application on the official Indian eVisa portal (indianvisaonline.gov.in).<br>3. <b>Receive ETA:</b> Once approved, you will receive an Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA) by email.<br>4. <b>Travel:</b> Present your ETA and Canadian passport at Indian immigration. The eVisa is not a visa on arrival.</p><p><b>Important Note:</b> <br>Your passport must be valid for at least 6 months from your date of entry into India and contain at least two blank pages.</p><p>Last Verified: October 26, 2026</p>
