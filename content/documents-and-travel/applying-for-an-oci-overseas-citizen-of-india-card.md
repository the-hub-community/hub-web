# Applying for an OCI (Overseas Citizen of India) Card

<div class="lh-content-body">

<div class="lh-section">


<div class="lh-text">
<p><b>Processing Time &amp; Costs</b></p><p><b>Processing Time:</b> Typically 6 to 8 weeks from the date of submission at a BLS center, though this can vary.</p><p><b>Fees (in Canada):</b> The new OCI registration fee is CAD 376 (includes a CAD 5 ICWF charge). BLS charges an additional CAD 1.70 processing fee plus courier charges.</p><p><b>Step-by-Step Process</b></p><p><b>Check Eligibility:</b> You are generally eligible if you were a citizen of India on or after January 26, 1950, or are a child/grandchild/great-grandchild of such a person.</p><p><b>Gather Required Documents:</b> <br><ul><li>Proof of current Canadian citizenship (Canadian passport).</li><li>Proof of Indian origin (e.g., previous Indian passport, birth certificate, or surrender certificate).</li><li>Proof of address in Canada. </li></ul><b>For minors:</b><br><ul><li> Birth certificate</li><li>Parents' passports</li><li>Proof of marital status</li></ul></p><p><b>Complete the Online Application:</b> Fill out the form on the Government of India's OCI Services Portal (<b>ociservices.gov.in</b>) and print it.</p><p><b>Pay the Fees:</b> Pay the required fees as per BLS instructions.</p><p><b>Book a BLS Appointment &amp; Submit in Person:</b> As of <b>April 5, 2026</b>, all OCI applications must be submitted in person at a BLS Centre for biometric enrollment. Postal submission is no longer accepted.</p><p><b>Track Your Application:</b> Monitor your status online via the BLS International portal.</p><p><b>Official Links &amp; Resources</b></p><p><ul><li>OCI Services Portal: ociservices.gov.in</li><li>BLS International Canada: blsindia-canada.com</li><li>High Commission of India, Ottawa: hciottawa.gov.in</li></ul></p><p>Last Verified: October 26, 2026</p>
