# Renewing Your Indian Passport in Canada

<div class="lh-content-body">

<div class="lh-section">


<div class="lh-text">
<p><b>Processing Time &amp; Costs</b></p><p>Standard Processing: Approximately 8 weeks for the Toronto and Ottawa jurisdictions; 2–4 weeks for Vancouver if your passport was issued there. These times exclude postal delays.</p><p>Tatkal (Expedited) Processing: 2 business days (plus shipping time) for renewal cases only.</p><p>Police Verification: If required, this can add several weeks to months to the timeline, as it involves checks in India.</p><p><b>Fees (Effective July 1, 2026)</b></p><p><ul><li>Adult 36-Page Booklet: CAD 176 + CAD 1.70 BLS service fee = CAD 177.70.</li><li>Adult 60-Page Booklet: CAD 246 + CAD 1.70 BLS fee.</li><li>Minor (Under 18): CAD 128 + CAD 1.70 BLS fee.</li><li>Tatkal Surcharge: An additional fee applies; the total for Tatkal is approximately CAD 349.</li></ul></p><p><b>Step-by-Step Process</b></p><p>Fill Out the Online Form: Complete the application on the Passport Seva Portal (<b>passportindia.gov.in</b>).</p><p>Gather Required Documents: Prepare the following: <br><ul><li>Printout of the online application form.</li><li>Original current Indian passport + photocopy of first and last pages.</li><li>Two recent photographs (51x51 mm, ICAO specifications).</li><li>Proof of Canadian status (PR card, work/study permit, visitor visa).</li><li>Proof of Canadian address (driver's license, utility bill).</li><li><b>Annexure 'E'</b> (self-declaration) for standard renewals.</li><li><b>Annexure 'D' </b>(for minors) signed by both parents, plus copies of parents' passports.</li><li><b>Annexure 'F'</b> and a police report if the previous passport was lost or damaged.</li></ul></p><p><b>Submit to BLS International:</b> You must submit your application through BLS. Find your nearest center at <b>blsindia-canada.com</b>. In-person and postal submissions are available.</p><p><b>Pay the Fees:</b> Pay the applicable fees at the BLS center.</p><p><b>Track Your Application:</b> You can track your application status online through the BLS website.</p><p>Official Links &amp; Resources</p><p>• Passport Seva Portal: passportindia.gov.in<br>• BLS International Canada: blsindia-canada.com<br>• Consulate General of India, Toronto: cgitoronto.gov.in</p><p><b>Important Note</b>: A new passport's validity is 10 years for adults and 5 years for minors. However, if you hold a visitor permit in Canada, your passport will only be issued for 2 years.</p><p>Last Verified: October 26, 2026</p>
