# India ↔ Canada

<div class="lh-content-body">

<div class="lh-section">


<div class="lh-text">
<p>A Complete Guide for Indian Families in Canada</p><p>Covering Passport Renewal, OCI Applications, Visa Options for Family Visits, and a Travel Checklist for Trips between Canada and India.</p><p>As an Indian family living in Canada, navigating the complexities of travel documents and visa requirements can be overwhelming. This guide aims to provide a comprehensive overview of the key steps involved in passport renewal, OCI applications, visa options for family visits, and a travel checklist for trips between Canada and India.</p><p>**Passport Renewal**</p><p>If you're a Canadian citizen of Indian origin, you may be eligible for a passport renewal. To apply for a passport renewal, you'll need to submit the following documents:</p><p>• A completed application form<br>• A photocopy of your current passport<br>• Proof of Canadian citizenship (birth certificate or citizenship certificate)<br>• Proof of Indian origin (birth certificate or parent's passport)<br>• Two recent passport-sized photographs</p><p>**OCI Applications**</p><p>If you're a Canadian citizen of Indian origin, you may be eligible for an Overseas Citizen of India (OCI) card. To apply for an OCI card, you'll need to submit the following documents:</p><p>• A completed application form<br>• A photocopy of your current passport<br>• Proof of Canadian citizenship (birth certificate or citizenship certificate)<br>• Proof of Indian origin (birth certificate or parent's passport)<br>• Two recent passport-sized photographs</p><p>**Visa Options for Family Visits**</p><p>If you're planning a family visit to India, you'll need to apply for a visa. There are several types of visas available, including:</p><p>• Tourist visa: valid for up to 6 months<br>• Business visa: valid for up to 1 year<br>• Student visa: valid for up to 5 years<br>• Employment visa: valid for up to 5 years</p><p>**Travel Checklist**</p><p>Before embarking on a trip between Canada and India, make sure you have the following documents:</p><p>• A valid passport<br>• A valid visa (if required)<br>• A photocopy of your passport<br>• A photocopy of your visa (if required)<br>• Travel insurance documents<br>• Flight itinerary<br>• Hotel reservation<br>• Contact information for your emergency contacts</p><p>By following this guide, you'll be well-prepared for your trip between Canada and India. Remember to check the official government websites for the latest information on passport renewal, OCI applications, and visa requirements.</p>
