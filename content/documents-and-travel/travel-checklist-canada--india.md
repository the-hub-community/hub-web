# Travel Checklist: Canada ↔ India

<div class="lh-content-body">

<div class="lh-section">


<div class="lh-text">
<p><b>For All Travellers:</b></p><p><ul><li>Valid passport (valid for at least 6 months beyond your return date).</li><li>Valid visa or OCI card for India.</li><li>Completed e-Arrival Card for India (can be submitted online within 72 hours before arrival).</li><li>Printed copy of your visa/ETA approval letter.</li><li>Proof of funds and return tickets.</li></ul></p><p><b>For Minors Travelling:</b></p><p><ul><li>Consent Letter: If a child is travelling with only one parent or a guardian, a notarized authorization letter from the non-travelling parent is strongly recommended for both leaving Canada and entering India.</li><li>Proof of Citizenship/Status: The child's Canadian passport, citizenship certificate, or PR card to re-enter Canada.</li><li>Birth Certificate: Carry the original copy.</li><li>Minor Visa Documents: A minor consent form, copies of both parents' passports, and a court order if applicable for single-parent custody.</li></ul></p><p><b>Airline &amp; Border Reminders:</b></p><p><ul><li>Check airline-specific baggage allowances and travel advisories.</li><li>Ensure all documents are in a carry-on bag for easy access at border crossings.</li><li>Familiarize yourself with CBSA (Canada Border Services Agency) and Indian customs regulations regarding goods and currency.</li></ul></p><p>Last Verified: October 26, 2026</p>
