# Free GTA Christmas Events Nov-Dec 2026 - Parades, Lights & Markets

<div class="lh-content-body">

<div class="lh-section">


<div class="lh-text">
Every event below is free. Dates run Nov 12, 2026 to Jan 1, 2027. Always check the official site before going — times and locations can change. Kids 12 and under ride the TTC free.<div><br></div><div>Parades</div><div>• Original Santa Claus Parade — Sun Nov 22, 12:30 pm, Christie Pits to St Lawrence Market, Toronto. Arrive early for a spot along the route and dress warmly — it is a long outdoor parade.</div><div>• Mississauga Santa Claus Parade — Sat Nov 21, 4–7 pm, Celebration Square, 300 City Centre Dr, Mississauga. Followed by Light Up the Square. The square is stroller-friendly and close to transit.</div><div>• Brampton Santa Claus Parade — Sat Nov 21, 5 pm, Bramalea Rd, Brampton. An evening parade — bundle up and bring something warm to drink.</div><div>• Brampton Tree Lighting — Sat Nov 28, 4–8 pm, Brampton. An evening event; check the City of Brampton site for the exact location before you go.</div><div><br></div><div>Lights</div><div>• Cavalcade of Lights — Sat Nov 28, 5–10 pm, Nathan Phillips Square, Toronto. The lights stay up until Jan 7. A good pick for infants and toddlers: it is outdoors, open, and stroller-friendly.</div><div>• Bloor-Yorkville Holiday Magic — Nov 21–Dec 31, free, Bloor-Yorkville, Toronto. Window displays and holiday lights you can walk through at your own pace.</div><div><br></div><div>Markets and Villages</div><div>• Holiday Fair in the Square — Dec 10–20, Sankofa Square, Toronto, free. A holiday market in the heart of downtown.</div><div>• Streetsville Christmas in the Village — Nov 20–21, Streetsville, Mississauga. A village-style Christmas event, good for a relaxed family stroll.</div><div>• Harbourfront Redpath Holiday Train — Nov 20–Dec 20, Fri–Sun, Harbourfront, Toronto, free. Weekend train fun for kids down at the waterfront.</div><div><br></div><div>Free Museums and More</div><div>• ROM Third Tuesday — Dec 15, 4–8:30 pm, free, advance ticket required. Book your free ticket ahead — the free evening fills up.</div><div>• Cineplex Community Day — Sat Nov 14, free movies at Cineplex theatres. Check your local theatre for showtimes and free ticket details.</div><div><br></div><div>Getting There — TTC and GO Tips</div><div>• TTC: kids 12 and under ride free. Keep little ones close on crowded parade days; stations near Nathan Phillips Square and St Lawrence Market can be busy.</div><div>• GO Transit: Mississauga, Brampton, and Vaughan are an easy GO train ride from Toronto — check GO schedules before you leave, especially for evening events.</div><div>• Arrive early for parades and tree lightings: the best spots go fast and roads close along parade routes.</div><div><br></div><div>What to Bring</div><div>• Warm layers, hats, and mittens for outdoor evening events.</div><div>• Snacks and water, especially for kids.</div><div>• A stroller for toddlers at open-air events like Cavalcade of Lights, Celebration Square, and Bloor-Yorkville.</div><div>• A fully charged phone for photos — and to double-check event times on the day.</div><div><br></div><div>Kids Tips</div><div>• Best for infants and toddlers: Cavalcade of Lights (open square), Holiday Fair in the Square, and daytime market strolls.</div><div>• Skip late-night parades in the cold with infants; afternoon events are easier on little ones.</div><div>• Verify every event on the official site — dates, times, and locations can change.</div>
