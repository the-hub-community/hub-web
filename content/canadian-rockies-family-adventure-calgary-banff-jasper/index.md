# Pre-Trip Planning & Essential Logistics

<div class="lh-content-body">

<div class="lh-section">


<div class="lh-text">
<p><strong>Parks Canada Discovery Pass</strong></p><ul><li><p><strong>What it is:</strong> A pass that covers admission to all Parks Canada national parks, including Banff and Jasper. It is valid for all seasons.</p></li><li><p><strong>2026 Fees:</strong> Adult: <strong>$83.50** | Senior: **$71.50</strong> | Family/Group (up to 7 people): <strong>$167.50</strong>. Children under 18 are free.</p></li><li><p><strong>Important Note:</strong> For a family of 9 (6 adults, 1 youth, 1 child, 1 infant), you will likely need to purchase a Family/Group pass plus additional adult passes, as the family pass covers up to 7 people. Verify the most cost-effective combination based on your group's composition.</p></li></ul><p><strong>Lake Louise &amp; Moraine Lake Shuttle Reservations</strong></p><ul><li><p><strong>Why you need it:</strong> Parking at these iconic lakes fills up very early, and road restrictions are in place. The Parks Canada shuttle is the best way to guarantee access.</p></li><li><p><strong>How to book:</strong> Reservations must be made through the <strong>Parks Canada Reservation Service</strong> (online or by phone at 1-877-737-3783).</p></li><li><p><strong>Crucial Tip:</strong> Shuttle seats are reservation-only and <strong>typically sell out well in advance</strong>. Do not attempt to secure a seat on the day of travel. Book as soon as your dates are confirmed.</p></li></ul><p><strong>Vehicle &amp; Car Seat Logistics</strong></p><ul><li><p>With 2 SUVs and 9 people, you will need to carefully plan your packing. Ensure you have the correct car seats or boosters for the youth and child, as required by Alberta law. The itinerary notes to "Install car seats" upon arrival at YYC. </p></li></ul><p><strong>Grocery &amp; Supply Stop</strong></p><ul><li><p>The itinerary includes a stop at <strong>IGA Canmore</strong> for groceries and supplies after picking up the rental cars. This is a smart move to stock up on snacks, drinks, and breakfast items for the Airbnb stays. </p></li></ul><p><em>Last Verified: October 26, 2026<br></em></p>
