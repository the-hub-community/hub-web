# Day 1 — Calgary → Jasper via the Icefields Parkway

<div class="lh-content-body">

<div class="lh-section">


<div class="lh-text">
<p><strong>The shape of the day:</strong> ~4.5 hours of driving, ~3 hours of visiting, and an infant who will spend most of the day in the car. This is the longest transit day; do not add stops beyond the four in your plan.</p><p><strong>08:31 — Arrive YYC and install car seats.</strong> Your companion document puts arrival at <strong>08:31</strong>; the first task at the airport is fitting car seats into both SUVs.</p><p><strong>10:00 — Rental pickup and grocery stop at IGA Canmore.</strong> Allow <strong>1.5 hours</strong> for the rental desk and <strong>30 minutes</strong> at IGA Canmore for snacks, picnic supplies, and infant food. Canmore is the last full-service grocery before Jasper; stock enough for the Bow Lake picnic and the drive.</p><p><strong>12:00 — Peyto Lake (45 min).</strong> A <strong>200 m paved trail</strong> makes this stroller-friendly — a rare win on this route. The viewing platform is one of the Parkway’s signature stops; the large lot is free.</p><p><strong>13:00 — Bow Lake (20 min, picnic lunch).</strong> Ten minutes from Peyto, with a free pull-off and space for the infant in a carrier. Eat here rather than at a restaurant — it keeps the day moving and avoids an unplanned hour-long stop.</p><p><strong>13:45 — Scenic drive to Jasper (3 hours).</strong> The infant naps in Car 2 during this leg. This is the stretch where wildlife sightings are most likely; assign one adult in each vehicle as the “wildlife spotter” and remind the drivers: <strong>slow down, hazard lights, pull over only where safe, never stop in the driving lane</strong>.</p><p><strong>17:30 — Athabasca Falls (45 min).</strong> Concrete railings make this safe for the 6-year-old, and the infant stays in a carrier. Large free lot.</p><p><strong>18:45 — Sunwapta Falls (45 min).</strong> Golden-hour light and a safe bridge; medium free lot. This is your last stop before Jasper.</p><p><strong>20:00 — Check in to Jasper Airbnb, 20:30 dinner at the Airbnb.</strong> Off-street parking for two vehicles and an easy evening — exactly what the group needs after a 12-hour day.<br></p>
