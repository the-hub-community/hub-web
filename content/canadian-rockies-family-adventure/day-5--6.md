# Day 5 & 6: Yoho National Park & Calgary City Highlights

<div class="lh-content-body">

<div class="lh-section">
<div class="lh-text">
**Overview:** The final two days of the trip combine the natural beauty of Yoho National Park with family-friendly fun in Calgary. This provides a balanced end to the adventure, with shorter drives and plenty of opportunities for kids to burn off energy.
</div>
<div class="lh-clear"></div>
</div>

<div class="lh-section">
<div class="lh-text">
**Day 5: Yoho National Park**
*   **Drive to Yoho:** A 1.5-hour drive from Calgary.
*   **Takakkaw Falls:** A spectacular waterfall. Bring raincoats, as the mist can be heavy. The path is stroller-friendly.
*   **Picnic Lunch:** Enjoy lunch at the falls.
*   **Emerald Lake:** A beautiful lake where you can walk the first 2 km with a stroller or rent a canoe (optional).
*   **Natural Bridge:** A quick photo stop to see the powerful Kicking Horse River.
*   **Drive back to Calgary:** 2-hour drive.
</div>
<div class="lh-clear"></div>
</div>

<div class="lh-section">
<div class="lh-text">
**Day 6: Calgary City Highlights & Departure**
*   **Drive to Johnston Canyon:** A 1.5-hour drive from Calgary.
*   **Johnston Canyon (Lower Falls):** This is a stroller-friendly walk on a boardwalk to a beautiful waterfall. It is a perfect final hike for the family.
*   **Drive to Calgary Tower:** Head into the city for the afternoon.
*   **Calgary Tower:** Kids will love the glass floor. An infant can be carried in a carrier.
*   **Stephen Avenue & Prince's Island Park:** A short walk for ice cream and a visit to a great playground with a river walk.
*   **Early Dinner:** Enjoy a final family meal before heading to the airport for your departure.
</div>
<div class="lh-clear"></div>
</div>

<div class="lh-section">
<div class="lh-text">
**Do's & Don'ts for Families**
*   **Do** pack rain gear for Yoho, even if it looks sunny.
*   **Do** choose the Lower Falls hike at Johnston Canyon for a family-friendly option.
*   **Do** let the kids run off energy at Prince's Island Park before the flight.
*   **Don't** attempt the full Upper Falls hike at Johnston Canyon if you have young children or a stroller.
*   **Don't** forget to leave extra time for returning your rental car and getting to the airport.
</div>
