# Canadian Rockies Family Travel Guide (Calgary – Banff – Jasper)

<div class="lh-content-body">

<div class="lh-section">
<div class="lh-text">
The Canadian Rockies are a breathtaking destination for families, but a successful trip requires careful planning. This guide breaks down a classic 6-day itinerary into manageable, standalone subpages. Each page is designed to be a 3–5 minute read, covering everything from critical pre-trip bookings to day-by-day activities, with specific tips for traveling with children and infants.

Whether you are navigating the shuttle system to Moraine Lake or preparing for a day on the Icefields Parkway, use these guides as your trusted travel companion. Start with the **Pre-Trip Checklist** to lock in your reservations, then follow the daily guides for a smooth and memorable family adventure.

### Wiki Pages in This Guide

- **[Pre-Trip Checklist: Bookings, Passes & Family Prep]**
  - Secure shuttle reservations, buy your park pass, book must-do activities, and prepare your vehicle and family for the journey.

- **[Day 1: Calgary to Jasper via the Icefields Parkway]**
  - A full-day scenic drive featuring Peyto Lake, Bow Lake, and the powerful Athabasca and Sunwapta Falls.

- **[Day 2: Jasper Highlights – Maligne Lake, SkyTram & Wildlife]**
  - A family-friendly day including a boat cruise to Spirit Island, a scenic tram ride, and an evening wildlife drive.

- **[Day 3: Jasper to Calgary via the Columbia Icefield]**
  - Experience the Athabasca Glacier on the Ice Explorer and walk the glass-floored Skywalk before heading back to Calgary.

- **[Day 4: Lake Louise, Moraine Lake & Banff Gondola]**
  - Use the Parks Canada shuttle to visit the iconic lakes, then soar up Sulphur Mountain for panoramic views.

- **[Day 5 & 6: Yoho National Park & Calgary City Highlights]**
  - Explore Takakkaw Falls and Emerald Lake before wrapping up your trip with family fun in Calgary at Johnston Canyon and Prince's Island Park.
