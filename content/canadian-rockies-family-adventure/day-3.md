# Day 3: Jasper to Calgary via the Columbia Icefield

<div class="lh-content-body">

<div class="lh-section">
<div class="lh-text">
**Overview:** This is a travel day that includes one of the trip's most unique experiences: walking on a glacier. Be prepared for cold and windy conditions, even in summer. The drive from Jasper to Calgary via the Icefields Parkway is about 5 hours, so this is a long day.
</div>
<div class="lh-clear"></div>
</div>

<div class="lh-section">
<div class="lh-text">
**Morning: Journey to the Glacier**
*   **Drive to Columbia Icefield:** A 1.5-hour drive from Jasper.
*   **Ice Explorer Truck:** Board a specialized vehicle for a 1.5-hour tour onto the Athabasca Glacier. **Dress everyone in warm layers, including snow pants, mitts, and toques.** Pre-booking is essential.
</div>
<div class="lh-clear"></div>
</div>

<div class="lh-section">
<div class="lh-text">
**Afternoon: Skywalk & Drive to Calgary**
*   **Lunch at Discovery Centre:** A warm, indoor meal is a welcome break after the glacier.
*   **Drive to Calgary:** 3.5-hour drive.
*   **Check-in to Calgary Accommodation:** Settle in and enjoy an easy dinner.
</div>
<div class="lh-clear"></div>
</div>

<div class="lh-section">
<div class="lh-text">
** Do's & Don'ts for Families**
*   **Do** pack a dedicated bag with winter gear for the glacier visit.
*   **Do** bring snacks and entertainment for the long drive to Calgary.
*   **Don't** underestimate the cold on the glacier. It is significantly colder than the townsite.
*   **Don't** forget to bring a carrier for the s, inf any, as strollers are not practical on the Ice Explorer or Skywalk.
</div>
