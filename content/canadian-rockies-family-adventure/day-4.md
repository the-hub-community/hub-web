# Day 4: Lake Louise, Moraine Lake & Banff Gondola

<div class="lh-content-body">

<div class="lh-section">
<div class="lh-text">
**Overview:** This is a big day that requires the shuttle reservations you made in advance. The itinerary involves two iconic lakes and an optional gondola ride. It is a full day (around 8 hours of activities) with significant time on shuttles.
</div>
<div class="lh-clear"></div>
</div>

<div class="lh-section">
<div class="lh-text">
**Morning: The Iconic Lakes**
*   **Shuttle Pickup:** Head to the shuttle stop.
*   **Lake Louise:** Explore the shoreline. The area is flat and stroller-friendly, making it easy to navigate with a family.
*   **Transfer to Moraine Lake:** The Lake Connector shuttle is included in the reservation.
*   **Moraine Lake:** The short paved trail to the Rockpile viewpoint offers the classic view. A stroller is okay on the main path.
</div>
<div class="lh-clear"></div>
</div>

<div class="lh-section">
<div class="lh-text">
**Afternoon & Evening: Banff Gondola & Hot Springs**
*   **Lunch in Banff:** A family-friendly restaurant in the townsite[reference:24].
*   **Banff Gondola:** The enclosed cabin is safe but strollers are left at the base.
*   **Banff Upper Hot Springs (Optional):** A relaxing way to end the day. Kids love the warm pools, and infants can dip their toes while being supervised.
*   **Drive back to Calgary:** The kids will likely sleep in the car on the way back.
</div>
<div class="lh-clear"></div>
</div>

<div class="lh-section">
<div class="lh-text">
** Do's & Don'ts for Families**
*   **Do** double-check your shuttle reservation times and location.
*   **Do** bring swimsuits and towels if you plan to visit the hot springs.
*   **Do** use the shuttle rides as a chance for everyone to rest.
*   **Don't** miss your shuttle departure window. Plan to arrive early.
*   **Don't** forget that strollers are not allowed on the gondola; bring a carrier for the infant, inf any.
</div>
