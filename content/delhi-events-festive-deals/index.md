**KIDS &amp; FAMILY OUTINGS**

- National Science Centre — near Pragati Maidan Gate No. 1 — open daily 9:30 AM–6:00 PM. Interactive galleries and 3D shows.
- India Gate, Kartavya Path &amp; Children's Park — evening family walks, open lawns and play areas. Nearest Metro: Central Secretariat / Khan Market.

**PRACTICAL LOCAL TIPS**

- Use the Delhi Metro whenever possible to skip event parking queues — Blue Line to Supreme Court for Pragati Maidan and the National Science Centre, Yellow Line for Saket and Central Secretariat.
- Visit Sunder Nursery and IITF on weekday mornings for lighter crowds and easier entry.
- Pack an extra layer for evening strolls at Kartavya Path or outdoor mall plazas once the sun sets.
