**THIS WEEKEND IN DELHI (NOV 20–22, 2026)**

- 45th India International Trade Fair (IITF 2026) at Bharat Mandapam, Pragati Maidan: Ongoing through Nov 27, with a Lucknow Gharana Kathak performance at Auditorium 2 on Friday, Nov 20 (6:00–7:30 PM). Take the Blue Line to Supreme Court Metro Station to skip the parking rush.
- Saras Food Festival at Sunder Nursery, Nizamuddin: Opens Nov 21 and runs through Dec 8 (11:30 AM–8:00 PM) — a lovely winter afternoon spot for regional food stalls; nearest Metro is Jangpura or JLN Stadium.
- TCS Ruhaniyat Sufi &amp; Mystic Music Festival at Bharat Mandapam Amphitheatre (Sunday, Nov 22) and 22nd SaMaPa Sangeet Sammelan at Kamani Auditorium, Mandi House (Nov 21–22, 5:45 PM; get off at Mandi House Metro).
- IITF 2026 Pragati Maidan walkthrough video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Jlxhk9GIMcQ

**UPCOMING MARKETS &amp; KIDS' OUTINGS**

- Select CITYWALK Saket &amp; DLF Promenade Vasant Kunj Winter/Christmas Markets: Pop-up festive markets coming up in December with free entry, holiday decor, and family dining (Malviya Nagar Metro for Saket or Vasant Vihar Metro for Vasant Kunj).
- National Science Centre (near Pragati Maidan Gate No. 1): Open daily 9:30 AM–6:00 PM — always a hit with kids for interactive galleries and 3D shows.
- India Gate / Kartavya Path &amp; Children's Park (Central Secretariat / Khan Market Metro) for easy evening family walks, plus Surajkund weekend outings (Badarpur / Tughlakabad Metro + auto) for a relaxed half-day trip.
- Select Citywalk Christmas &amp; New Year setup video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q-bkm16gdyU

**KIDS &amp; FAMILY OUTINGS&nbsp;**- National Science Centre (near Pragati Maidan Gate No. 1): Open daily 9:30 AM–6:00 PM with interactive science exhibits and 3D shows.

- India Gate, Kartavya Path &amp; Children’s Park: Classic evening walks, open lawns, and play areas for kids.

- Surajkund Weekend Outings: Scenic day trips and a heads-up for the annual Surajkund Crafts Mela coming up in early February.

**PRACTICAL LOCAL TIPS&nbsp;**- Use the Delhi Metro whenever possible (Blue Line to Supreme Court for Pragati Maidan/National Science Centre, Yellow Line for Saket/Central Secretariat) to skip event parking queues.

- Visit Sunder Nursery and IITF on weekday mornings for lighter crowds and easier entry.

- Pack an extra layer for evening strolls at Kartavya Path or outdoor mall plazas once the sun sets.
