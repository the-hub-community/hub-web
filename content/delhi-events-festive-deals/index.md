<div class="lh-text">
**PRAGATI MAIDAN &amp; BHARAT MANDAPAM**- 45th India International Trade Fair (IITF 2026): Running Nov 13–27, 2026 across 17 halls with state pavilions, crafts, and food stalls.
- ACETECH Delhi: Dec 17–20, 2026 for architecture, design, and building materials showcases.
- IITF 2026 Pragati Maidan tour video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Jlxhk9GIMcQ

<div class="lh-section">


<div class="lh-text">
**WINTER &amp; CHRISTMAS MARKETS**- Sunder Nursery Winter &amp; Christmas Markets: Open Nov 21–Dec 8 plus December weekend markets — great for artisanal gifts, bakery stalls, and daytime picnics.
- Select CITYWALK Saket Christmas &amp; New Year Carnival: Mid-to-late December with free entry, festive decor, and live performances.
- DLF Promenade Vasant Kunj Winter Markets: Outdoor winter pop-ups, holiday shopping, and family-friendly dining.
- Select Citywalk Christmas &amp; New Year setup video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q-bkm16gdyU
</div>
<div class="lh-clear"></div>
</div>

<div class="lh-section">


<div class="lh-text">
**KIDS &amp; FAMILY OUTINGS**- National Science Centre (near Pragati Maidan Gate No. 1): Open daily 9:30 AM–6:00 PM with interactive science exhibits and 3D shows.
- National Science Centre reference image: Delhi Tourism (national_science.jpg)
- India Gate, Kartavya Path &amp; Children's Park: Classic evening walks, open lawns, and play areas for kids.
- Surajkund Weekend Outings: Scenic day trips and a heads-up for the annual Surajkund Crafts Mela coming up in early February.
</div>
<div class="lh-clear"></div>
</div>

<div class="lh-section">


<div class="lh-text">
**PRACTICAL LOCAL TIPS**- Use the Delhi Metro whenever possible (Blue Line to Supreme Court for Pragati Maidan/National Science Centre, Yellow Line for Saket/Central Secretariat) to skip event parking queues.
- Visit Sunder Nursery and IITF on weekday mornings for lighter crowds and easier entry.
- Pack an extra layer for evening strolls at Kartavya Path or outdoor mall plazas once the sun sets.
