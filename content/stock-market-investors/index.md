**Welcome to Stock Market Investors**

Stock Market Investors is a community for people who follow the stock market worldwide. It brings together daily market analysis, portfolios, and learning in one place, so members can follow market moves, compare ideas, and build a clearer understanding over time.

Whether you follow markets closely or check in once a week, this page is a starting point for reading analysis, looking at portfolio ideas, and learning how members think about companies and sectors.

**What this community covers**

- Daily market analysis: what moved markets, which sectors stood out, and what news mattered.

- Portfolios: ideas grouped by style, from large established companies to growth, income, and broad market funds.

- Learning: how markets work, how to read company results, and how to think about risk and valuation.

- A worldwide view: markets in North America and around the world, and how events in one region can affect others.

**How to use it**

1) Start with the market, not a single stock - index, sector, news. Begin with the broad picture before focusing on one company.

2) Check earnings, revenue trend, debt, dividend and valuation before price. A price on its own tells only part of the story.

3) Track a watchlist, write why, at what price, and what would change your mind. Keep a short list and note why each company interests you.

4) Review weekly, not every minute. A weekly review is enough to stay informed without constant checking.

**Portfolios**

This community groups portfolio ideas into four styles: Bluechips, Growth, Dividends, and Index / ETFs. Each has its own sub-page, starting with Bluechips "Bluechip Portfolios - Top 10 Large Stocks Down 35-55% in the Last 12 Months".

Bluechips focuses on large, established companies. Growth looks at companies expanding revenue and earnings. Dividends focuses on companies that return cash to shareholders. Index / ETFs looks at broad funds that track a market or sector.

**How to contribute**

Add a stock in the comments with ticker, exchange, date, source link. Page updates go for review, comments appear right away. Include the ticker, the exchange, the date, and a link to your source, so other members can verify it.

**What not to post**

- No guaranteed returns

- No personal tips

- No names
