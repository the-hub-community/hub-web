# Local Parks & Trails

<div class="lh-content-body">

<div class="lh-section">
<div class="lh-text">
**Kariya Park** is the crown jewel of our neighborhood. Inspired by Japanese gardens, it features a tranquil koi pond, a zig-zag boardwalk, and a stunning cascade waterfall. In the spring, the cherry blossom trees transform the park into a pink wonderland—it's a must-see for any resident.

For those looking for something more active, the **Waterfront Trail** is just a 10-minute walk east. It offers 5km of paved paths perfect for jogging, cycling, or a leisurely evening stroll.
