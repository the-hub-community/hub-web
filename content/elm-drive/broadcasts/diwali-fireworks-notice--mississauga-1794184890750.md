For Diwali on Sunday, Nov. 8, 2026, consumer fireworks are allowed only on private residential property from 6–10 p.m. No fireworks in parks, streets, sidewalks, parking lots, temples, or other public property. You must be 18+ and buy only from licensed retailers during Oct. 29–Nov. 7—not on Diwali day. Using another person’s property requires written permission. Display fireworks need a permit. Keep water nearby, soak used fireworks, and consider neighbours, pets, and noise. Fireworks cause noise and pollution; choose sparklers or LED lights if possible. Report violations or ask questions: call 311. Celebrate safely and respectfully. Happy Diwali!
