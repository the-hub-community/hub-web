Brampton has a blanket ban on all fireworks, including on Diwali. The prohibition covers the use, sale, purchase, possession, distribution, and discharge of consumer and display fireworks. Sparklers are the only exception and remain permitted. Violations carry fines starting at 1,000 for selling or distributing; property owners can also face penalties if fireworks are set off on their land. Residents are encouraged to attend City-led Diwali celebrations with professional pyrotechnics instead. Celebrate safely, respectfully, and in line with the bylaw. Happy Diwali!
