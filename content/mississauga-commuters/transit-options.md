# Getting to Union Station in the Morning

<div class="lh-content-body">

<div class="lh-section">
<div class="lh-text">
If you are commuting to downtown Toronto, here is a breakdown of the best options from the Mississauga area.

### 1. The GO Train (Milton Line) - *Highly Recommended*
The fastest and most reliable way to get downtown. 
*   **Stations:** Cooksville GO or Erindale GO.
*   **Travel Time:** Approximately 30–35 minutes to Union Station.
*   **Frequency:** Trains run roughly every 30 minutes during peak morning hours (6 AM to 9 AM).
*   **Cost:** Roughly $8.50 one-way (presto). 
*   **Pro Tip:** If you board at Cooksville, try to sit in the first 3 cars—it drops you closest to the York Concourse exit at Union.

### 2. MiWay to Kipling Subway
If the GO Train schedule doesn't work for you, you can take a MiWay bus to Kipling Station and transfer to the TTC Line 2 (Bloor-Danforth).
*   **Travel Time:** 50–70 minutes (depending on traffic).
*   **Cost:** One MiWay fare ($3.10) + TTC fare ($3.30).
*   **Best for:** Commuters who don't want to rely on the strict GO Train schedule.

### 3. Driving / Rideshare
*   **Travel Time:** 35 minutes on a good day, up to 90 minutes in heavy rain or snow.
*   **Cost:** $20+ for parking downtown, plus gas.
*   **Verdict:** Generally not recommended for daily commuting due to the QEW/Gardiner traffic and extreme parking costs.
