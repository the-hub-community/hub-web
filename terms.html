Terms of use

Be a good neighbour.

The Hub Community is a shared space built by residents. Using it means agreeing to keep it useful, honest, and civil.

What you agree to
  • Post only what you know to be true, and what you have the right to share.
  • Treat other residents with respect — no harassment, hate speech, or scams.
  • Don't post private information about other people without their consent.
  • Don't use the platform for commercial spam or unrelated promotion.
What we do
  • Every submission is reviewed before it goes live.
  • Content that violates these terms is removed without notice.
  • We can't verify every listing or claim — always use your own judgement.
  • The service is provided as-is, without warranty.
Report a problem

Every page and listing has a Report Content button. Reports are reviewed by moderators and are anonymous.
